About

I have a habit of chasing unreasonable ideas. A few turn beautiful. the rest become educational.

It’s also more or less how I move through the world; I’m still a little lost in the world, which I’ve come to suspect is the natural state of anyone paying attention. Along the way, I try to give back wherever curiousity may lead me. If you see me contributing to your project, it probably means it inspired me.
